# Website CRO Examples

## Example 1, Homepage for a service studio

### Goal

Get qualified leads to book a discovery call.

### Recommended structure

1. Hero
2. Trust bar
3. Services overview
4. Why choose us
5. Process
6. Results or testimonials
7. FAQ
8. Final CTA

### Hero example

Headline:
Websites built to look sharp, load fast, and drive action.

Subtext:
Flammin Studio designs high converting websites and landing pages that combine modern UI, clear messaging, and performance focused structure.

CTA:
Book a strategy call

Reasoning:
The headline communicates design, performance, and conversion in one line.
The subtext explains the service clearly.
The CTA matches a service based conversion path.

## Example 2, Landing page for a lead generation offer

### Goal

Generate quote requests.

### Recommended structure

1. Hero with direct offer
2. Trust signals
3. Pain points
4. Solution
5. What is included
6. FAQ
7. Form section
8. Final CTA

### Hero example

Headline:
Need a landing page that actually gets people to take action

Subtext:
We build focused landing pages with strong messaging, clean UX, and clear calls to action so your traffic has a better chance of converting.

CTA:
Request a free audit

Reasoning:
This is stronger for colder traffic.
The CTA lowers friction.
The wording is outcome focused.

## Example 3, Service section rewrite

### Weak version

We provide innovative digital experiences tailored to modern businesses.

### Stronger version

We design and build websites, landing pages, and digital experiences that help businesses present their offer clearly and turn more visitors into real leads.

Reasoning:
The stronger version says what is done, who it helps, and why it matters.

---

## Example 4, CTA rewrite

### Weak

Learn more

### Better

See how the process works

### Better for high intent

Start your project

### Better for lead generation

Get a custom quote

Reasoning:
CTA strength should match intent and stage.

## Example 5, Objection handling block

Headline:
Not sure where to start

Copy:
We guide you from the first strategy step to launch, with a clear process that keeps the project focused, fast, and easy to understand.

Reasoning:
This reduces hesitation for clients who fear technical confusion.
