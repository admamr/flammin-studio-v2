# Website CRO Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing any homepage, landing page, or service page.

## Goal Clarity

- Is the page goal clear
- Is there one primary action
- Is the audience clear
- Is the offer understandable in seconds

## Hero

- Does the hero clearly explain what is being offered
- Does the headline communicate value, not fluff
- Does the subtext support the headline
- Is the CTA visible above the fold
- Does the visual support understanding

## CTA

- Is there one dominant CTA
- Is the CTA action based and clear
- Is the CTA repeated logically through the page
- Are secondary CTAs non competing

## Trust

- Is trust introduced early enough
- Are there proof elements that feel real
- Are there credibility markers near moments of hesitation

## Offer Structure

- Are services or deliverables explained clearly
- Are features translated into outcomes
- Is the offer more specific than competitors’ generic messaging

## Objections

- Does the page address likely user doubts
- Is there a process section if uncertainty is high
- Is there an FAQ if friction needs reduction

## Forms

- Is the form short enough for the page intent
- Are fields necessary
- Are labels clear
- Is the form easy to complete on mobile

## Mobile

- Is the hero readable on mobile
- Is the CTA visible and usable on mobile
- Do cards stack cleanly
- Is spacing clean and not exhausting
- Is the page easy to scan with a thumb

## Copy

- Are headlines specific
- Are paragraphs concise
- Is the wording outcome focused
- Has vague marketing fluff been removed

## Final Flow

- Does the page move logically from attention to trust to offer to action
- Does every section earn its place
- Does the page feel built to convert, not just to impress
