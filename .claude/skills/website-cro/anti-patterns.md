# Website CRO Anti Patterns

Claude must avoid the following:

## Hero mistakes

- vague headlines
- no clear offer
- weak or missing CTA
- visual overload
- too much text above the fold

## CTA mistakes

- multiple equal priority CTAs
- unclear CTA wording
- no CTA repetition
- low intent CTA when page expects high intent action
- high commitment CTA too early without enough trust

## Structure mistakes

- adding sections to fill space
- random ordering of sections
- trust appearing too late
- no objection handling
- no explanation of process when needed

## Copy mistakes

- agency fluff
- buzzword stacking
- feature dumping without benefit explanation
- unclear audience targeting
- generic claims with no proof

## Form mistakes

- too many fields
- hidden form on long pages
- poor mobile form layout
- placeholder only labels
- no supporting trust cue

## Mobile mistakes

- desktop first layout squeezed into mobile
- giant text blocks
- hard to tap buttons
- cramped cards
- poor spacing rhythm

## Design mistakes that hurt CRO

- animation before clarity
- visual complexity that weakens comprehension
- low contrast CTA buttons
- overdesigned sections with weak messaging
- decorative layouts that hide important information

## Strategic mistakes

- trying to speak to everyone
- unclear value proposition
- no differentiation
- no proof
- no reason to act now
