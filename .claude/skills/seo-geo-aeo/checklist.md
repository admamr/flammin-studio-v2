# SEO GEO AEO Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing a page or article.

## Intent

- Is the page goal clear
- Is the page targeting one clear primary topic
- Does the content match the user intent
- Is the next step clear

## Structure

- Is there one clear H1
- Are H2 and H3 headings meaningful
- Can the page be scanned easily
- Does each section serve a real purpose

## Topic Clarity

- Is the page topic obvious early
- Is the service, concept, or article subject clearly defined
- Are relevant entities clear and natural
- Is branding supportive rather than distracting

## Metadata

- Is the title specific and aligned with intent
- Is the meta description useful and compelling
- Do both reflect the actual page honestly

## Content Quality

- Does the page answer real questions
- Is the writing concise and useful
- Has fluff been removed
- Are direct answer blocks included where helpful

## FAQ

- Are FAQ questions real and relevant
- Do FAQ answers add clarity
- Is FAQ useful enough to justify schema if used

## Internal Linking

- Are related pages linked where appropriate
- Is anchor text descriptive
- Does linking support topic clusters and user flow

## GEO and AEO

- Are important ideas stated directly
- Is the page easy to summarize or extract answers from
- Are entity relationships clear
- Is the writing understandable by both users and machines

## Schema

- Is schema appropriate for the page
- Does schema match visible content
- Is there any unsupported or misleading structured data

## Local Relevance

- If local, is the location clear and natural
- Are location references useful rather than stuffed
- Is the page genuinely relevant to that geography

## Trust

- Are claims realistic and precise
- Does the page feel credible
- Is the business or author clear where needed

## Final Review

- Does the page feel useful first and optimized second
- Would a human trust and understand this page quickly
- Is the page ready for search and AI interpretation without sounding robotic
