# Approved Motion Patterns

## 1. Hero Reveal

Use when:

- the page needs a premium first impression
- the hero contains headline, copy, CTA, and media

Pattern:

- headline enters first
- body copy follows
- CTA appears quickly after
- media enters with slight delay or parallel timing

Behavior:

- short movement distance
- opacity plus transform
- no long intro

---

## 2. Section Reveal

Use when:

- entering new content sections
- reinforcing scroll rhythm

Pattern:

- section content fades in with slight upward movement
- reveal happens near viewport entry
- timing remains short

Behavior:

- subtle y translation
- no exaggerated motion
- no slow fade drags

---

## 3. Stagger Grid

Use when:

- revealing service cards
- revealing features
- showing portfolio items

Pattern:

- group container enters
- children reveal with light stagger

Behavior:

- short stagger interval
- minimal travel distance
- consistent visual rhythm

---

## 4. CTA Emphasis

Use when:

- drawing light attention to the main CTA after headline reveal

Pattern:

- small fade and y movement
- optional tiny scale in

Behavior:

- quick
- non theatrical
- must not outshine headline clarity

---

## 5. Accordion State Motion

Use when:

- expanding FAQ or content panels

Pattern:

- smooth open close transition
- state change feels clear

Behavior:

- short
- readable
- no dramatic flourish

---

## 6. Image or Media Reveal

Use when:

- a supporting visual enters the hero or section

Pattern:

- opacity and small translate or scale
- reveal should support layout, not dominate

Behavior:

- elegant
- clean
- not oversized or cinematic unless brand calls for it
