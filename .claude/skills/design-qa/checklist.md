# Design QA Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing any page.

## Goal and Structure

- Is the page goal obvious
- Is the target audience clear
- Is there one primary CTA
- Does the section order support trust and action
- Does each section earn its place

## Hero

- Is the hero understandable in seconds
- Does the headline say something real
- Does the subtext support the message
- Is the CTA visible and strong
- Does the visual help, not distract

## Visual Hierarchy

- Is there a clear focal point
- Do headings stand out enough
- Is supporting text visually secondary
- Does the page guide the eye naturally

## Spacing and Layout

- Is section spacing consistent
- Is internal spacing clean
- Is content width readable
- Is alignment consistent
- Does the page breathe without wasting space

## Typography

- Is text readable across breakpoints
- Is the heading scale clear
- Are paragraphs comfortably short
- Is line height helping readability
- Is there enough contrast between text levels

## CTA and Conversion

- Is one CTA dominant
- Is CTA language action based
- Is CTA repeated logically
- Are trust and objection handling present where needed

## Forms

- Are forms short enough
- Are labels clear
- Is the form easy on mobile
- Is the submit action clear
- Is trust support nearby if needed

## Responsiveness

- Does mobile feel intentional
- Do layouts stack cleanly
- Are buttons easy to tap
- Is nav usable on smaller screens
- Is there any overflow or broken alignment

## Accessibility

- Is the HTML semantic
- Are labels present
- Are focus states visible
- Are interactions keyboard friendly
- Is contrast acceptable

## SEO and Semantics

- Is there one clear H1
- Are H2 and H3 headings meaningful
- Is the page topic obvious early
- Are FAQ opportunities used well if relevant
- Is the page easy to scan and extract from

## Motion

- Does motion help clarity
- Is motion subtle enough
- Does it avoid delaying content
- Is reduced motion respected
- Does it feel aligned with the design direction

## Consistency

- Are buttons consistent
- Are cards consistent
- Is spacing rhythm consistent
- Does the whole page feel like one system

## Maintainability

- Is the markup clean
- Is Tailwind usage manageable
- Is JS logic readable
- Would future edits be straightforward
