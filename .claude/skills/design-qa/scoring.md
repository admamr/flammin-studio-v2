# Design QA Scoring

Use this scoring guide when reviewing a page.

## 9 to 10

Strong production quality.
Clear, conversion ready, responsive, consistent, and refined.
Only minor polish opportunities remain.

## 7 to 8

Solid base with noticeable issues.
Good direction overall, but some weaknesses reduce perceived quality or effectiveness.

## 5 to 6

Mixed quality.
Some parts work, but important issues exist in clarity, hierarchy, responsiveness, or consistency.

## 3 to 4

Weak.
The page may look partially modern but has structural or usability problems that limit performance.

## 1 to 2

Poor.
The page is unclear, inconsistent, weak on mobile, or broken in key areas.

---

## Category Scoring

Score each area:

- goal clarity
- hero clarity
- CTA strength
- hierarchy
- spacing
- typography
- responsiveness
- accessibility
- SEO structure
- motion quality, if used
- consistency
- maintainability

Then summarize:

- overall score
- highest risk issues
- fastest high impact fixes
