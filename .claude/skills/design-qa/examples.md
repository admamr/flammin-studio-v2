# Design QA Examples

## Example 1, Hero audit

Issue:
The hero headline says “Modern digital experiences for ambitious brands” and the CTA says “Learn more.”

Why it is weak:

- vague headline
- unclear offer
- weak CTA wording
- looks polished but says little

Better direction:

- make the service or outcome explicit
- replace weak CTA with action based CTA
- support with one clear subheading

---

## Example 2, Mobile audit

Issue:
A 3 column service grid collapses poorly on mobile, cards feel cramped, and the CTA button width is inconsistent.

Severity:
Critical

Why it matters:

- hard to scan
- weak tap comfort
- reduces perceived quality
- hurts conversion flow

Fix direction:

- simplify stacking
- improve spacing
- standardize button width and padding
- reduce card density

---

## Example 3, SEO structure audit

Issue:
The page uses decorative section titles like “Built Different” and “Our Edge” without clearly stating the actual service.

Severity:
Medium

Why it matters:

- weaker semantic clarity
- harder for search systems to understand topic
- less clear for first time users

Fix direction:

- rewrite headings to reflect real service meaning
- preserve style in subtext if needed
- keep one clear H1 and logical H2s

---

## Example 4, Motion audit

Issue:
Hero content fades in too slowly, CTA appears late, and each section animates separately with heavy scroll delay.

Severity:
Critical

Why it matters:

- delays comprehension
- slows user trust building
- creates friction before action

Fix direction:

- speed up hero reveal
- show CTA earlier
- reduce scroll trigger count
- keep motion focused on key sections only

---

## Example 5, Consistency audit

Issue:
The page uses three different button radii, two card shadow styles, and inconsistent section padding.

Severity:
Medium

Why it matters:

- lowers premium feel
- weakens system consistency
- makes the site feel patched together

Fix direction:

- define one button system
- unify card styling
- standardize vertical rhythm
