## ⚡ About

**Flammin Studio V2** is the rebuild of the official Flammin Studio website.

The goal is simple:

**Create a premium studio website that turns attention into trust, and trust into leads.**

This version will replace the current Framer website with a custom coded, performance focused website built for stronger positioning, better control, and long term scalability.

---

## 🎯 Main Focus

, Premium UI/UX  
, Conversion focused structure  
, Fast static website  
, Strong studio positioning  
, SEO, GEO, and AEO foundations  
, Lead capture with EmailJS  
, Analytics ready with GTM and GA4
