You are working on the official Flammin Studio V2 website.

This project is a premium, conversion focused, custom-coded performance website with intentional motion and micro-interactions for Flammin Studio.

## Core Objective

Build a high performance studio website that positions Flammin Studio as a sharp web strategy, UI/UX, front end, SEO/GEO/AEO, and conversion partner for founders, service brands, and growth teams.

This is not a generic portfolio website.

This is a business development asset built to generate trust, leads, and strategic opportunities.

## Working Style

## Output Rules

## Motion Direction

Use motion to support clarity, rhythm, and conversion.

Allowed motion:
, Smooth section reveals
, Hover interactions
, Magnetic or elevated CTA behavior
, Subtle background movement
, Scroll-based storytelling where useful
, Case study card interactions
, Premium menu transitions
, Light GSAP usage only when it improves the experience

Avoid:
, Random animations
, Heavy effects that slow the site
, Overdone parallax
, Motion that distracts from the CTA
, Template-like animation presets
, Animating every element for no reason

## Important

The website should feel like a serious studio built by someone who understands design, conversion, search, analytics, and front end execution.

It should not feel like a template.
