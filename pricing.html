Standard foundation

Every build starts with the business layer.

Whether it is a single landing page or a full studio website, these principles and practices are part of every serious engagement.

  • Positioning clarity

    The offer, audience, and value proposition are defined before design begins.

  • CTA hierarchy

    Primary and secondary actions are structured to guide visitors toward the intended outcome.

  • Responsive UX

    Mobile-first implementation that works cleanly from 320px to large desktop.

  • Semantic HTML

    Clean, accessible markup that supports screen readers, crawlers, and long-term maintainability.

  • SEO-ready structure

    Heading hierarchy, metadata, canonical tags, and Open Graph coverage from day one.

  • Performance awareness

    Lightweight code, optimized assets, and no unnecessary libraries added to the build.

  • Analytics readiness

    Data attributes and event hooks structured for GTM and GA4 tracking from launch.

  • Launch QA

    Cross-device review, link checks, form testing, and final performance pass before delivery.
