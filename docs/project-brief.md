# 🔥 Flammin Studio V2, Project Brief

## Project Purpose

Flammin Studio V2 is the rebuild of the official Flammin Studio website.

The website is being rebuilt from the ground up as a custom coded, high performance, conversion focused studio website with intentional motion and premium visual direction.

The goal is to replace the current Framer based website with a stronger, faster, more controllable, and more scalable web presence.

This is not a regular portfolio refresh.

This is a business development asset built to generate trust, leads, credibility, and strategic opportunities.

## Core Objective

Build a premium studio website that positions Flammin Studio as a strategic web partner for founders, service brands, startups, and growth teams.

The site must clearly communicate that Flammin Studio helps businesses turn websites and landing pages into conversion systems.

## Core Positioning

Flammin Studio builds conversion focused websites, landing pages, and digital systems for founders, service brands, and growth teams that need sharper positioning, stronger UX, and measurable lead flow.

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

, startup founders  
, service brand owners  
, growth teams  
, lean marketing teams  
, agencies looking for web execution partners  
, operators preparing for campaigns, launches, investor conversations, or market expansion  

Secondary audience:

, local and international business owners  
, strategic partners  
, event and networking leads  
, potential remote work or collaboration opportunities  

## Main Business Goal

Turn website visitors into qualified leads.

The website should move visitors toward one primary action:

, booking a conversion audit  
, requesting a website review  
, starting a project inquiry  
, contacting Flammin Studio  

## Primary CTA

Preferred primary CTA:

> Book a Conversion Audit

Alternative CTA options:

, Request a Website Audit  
, Start a Project  
, Get a Website Review  

Use one primary CTA consistently across the site.

## Secondary CTA

Preferred secondary CTA:

> View Selected Work

Alternative secondary CTA options:

, See the Process  
, Explore Services  

The secondary CTA should support exploration, not compete with the primary CTA.

## Strategic Message

The site should make visitors feel:

, Flammin Studio understands business, not only design  
, the studio can sharpen positioning and user flow  
, the studio can build premium digital presence  
, the studio understands SEO, GEO, AEO, tracking, and conversion  
, the studio can move faster than a bloated agency  
, the studio is serious enough for founders and growth teams  

## What The Site Must Communicate

, Flammin Studio builds conversion focused websites and landing pages  
, the work is strategic, not decorative  
, the studio understands UI/UX, front end development, SEO/GEO/AEO, and analytics  
, the website is treated as a business system  
, every section has a role in the conversion journey  
, the studio has taste, execution ability, and technical control  

## What The Site Must Avoid

, looking like a generic agency template  
, sounding like a regular freelancer portfolio  
, overloading visitors with too many services  
, using vague creative language  
, relying only on visuals without business clarity  
, fake metrics  
, unnecessary animations  
, random 3D visuals  
, weak CTAs  
, cluttered layouts  
, overused SaaS design patterns

## Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

, sharp  
, premium  
, focused  
, strategic  
, confident  
, slightly aggressive  
, technically capable  
, creatively distinct  
, business minded  

The tone should be direct and clear.

No empty hype.

No soft agency language.

No corporate boredom.

## Visual Direction Summary

The visual direction should be:

, dark editorial  
, high contrast  
, premium  
, futuristic but mature  
, grid based  
, bold but controlled  
, motion enhanced  
, conversion focused  

The site should feel alive, but not chaotic.

## Technical Direction

Use:

, HTML  
, CSS  
, Tailwind CSS if needed  
, Vanilla JavaScript  
, GSAP only when needed  
, EmailJS  
, GTM  
, GA4  
, GSC  

Avoid:

, React unless explicitly requested  
, Next.js unless explicitly requested  
, CMS logic for the MVP  
, database logic for the MVP  
, backend complexity for the MVP  
, heavy animation dependencies  

## Success Criteria

The website is successful if it:

, explains Flammin Studio clearly within the first screen  
, creates trust fast  
, feels premium and non generic  
, has a strong conversion path  
, works perfectly on mobile  
, loads fast  
, supports analytics tracking  
, supports SEO/GEO/AEO structure  
, makes Flammin look ready for serious clients, founders, and partners
