# 🔥 Flammin Studio V2, SEO Map

## Purpose

This document defines the SEO, GEO, AEO, metadata, schema, content clarity, and analytics readiness direction for Flammin Studio V2.

The goal is to make the website understandable to:

, users  
, Google  
, AI search systems  
, answer engines  
, crawlers  
, screen readers  

The site must not only look premium.

It must be structured, searchable, readable, and conversion ready.

## SEO Objective

Build a clear search foundation for Flammin Studio as a conversion focused web studio.

The website should support visibility around:

, conversion focused websites  
, landing page design  
, web design for startups  
, web design for service brands  
, UI/UX web design  
, front end development  
, SEO ready websites  
, analytics ready websites  
, CRO landing pages  
, website conversion audits  
, SEO, GEO, and AEO website strategy  

## Core Search Positioning

Flammin Studio should be positioned as:

> A conversion focused web studio building websites, landing pages, and digital systems for founders, service brands, startups, and growth teams.

The site should avoid vague creative positioning and use clear service language that search engines and users can understand.

, responsive dimensions

, finalfinal.jpg  
, designnew.png

## GEO and AEO Direction

The website should be easy for AI systems and answer engines to understand.

Use:

, direct service descriptions  
, clear questions and answers  
, structured headings  
, specific language  
, concise explanations  
, proof based claims  
, visible FAQ content  
, semantic HTML  

Avoid:

, vague poetic copy  
, unclear service naming  
, clever wording that hides meaning  
, buzzword stacking  
, long abstract paragraphs  

AEO goal:

The site should clearly answer:

, What does Flammin Studio do?  
, Who is Flammin Studio for?  
, What services does Flammin Studio provide?  
, Does Flammin Studio design and build websites?  
, Does Flammin Studio handle SEO and analytics?  
, Can Flammin Studio improve an existing website?  
, How can someone contact Flammin Studio?
