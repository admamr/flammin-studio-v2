# 🔥 Flammin Studio V2, Design Direction

## Purpose

This document defines the visual and interaction direction for Flammin Studio V2.

The goal is to create a premium, conversion focused studio website that feels sharp, intentional, high end, and technically capable.

The design should not feel like a generic agency template, SaaS landing page, or AI generated dark website.

Flammin Studio V2 should feel like a serious digital studio with a clear point of view, strong execution, and conversion discipline.

## Design Thesis

Flammin Studio V2 should combine:

, dark editorial direction  
, premium visual hierarchy  
, sharp typography  
, controlled red signal accents  
, conversion focused layout  
, founder facing clarity  
, subtle but memorable motion  
, strong mobile experience  

The website should feel alive, but not noisy.

The design must support trust, clarity, and lead generation.

## Visual Keywords

The website should feel:

, premium  
, sharp  
, dark  
, editorial  
, futuristic  
, mature  
, strategic  
, confident  
, conversion focused  
, visually distinct  
, high contrast  
, controlled  
, intentional  

It should not feel:

, generic  
, soft  
, corporate boring  
, overly trendy  
, template based  
, startup SaaS default  
, cluttered  
, childish  
, over animated  
, AI generated  

## Brand Color System

### Primary Colors

```txt
Dark Base: #1D1616
Primary Red: #D84040
Deep Red: #8E1616
Light Text: #EEEEEE
```

### Color Usage

Use the dark base as the main environment.

Use light text for readability.

Use the primary red as a signal color for:

, primary CTA  
, active states  
, key highlights  
, small accents  
, section markers  
, hover states  
, important labels  

Use deep red for:

, secondary accents  
, gradient depth  
, borders  
, subtle backgrounds  
, hover layering  

Do not overuse red.

The red should feel intentional, like a signal, not decoration.

## Typography Direction

### Primary Font

```txt
Space Grotesk
```

Use for:

, headings  
, body copy  
, navigation  
, buttons  
, service cards  
, case study cards  

### Accent Fonts

```txt
Orbitron
Electrolize
```

Use only for:

, small labels  
, eyebrow text  
, metadata  
, technical badges  
, section numbers  
, micro UI accents  

Do not use futuristic fonts for long body copy.

Do not overuse Orbitron.

Typography must stay readable, premium, and controlled.

## Typography Behavior

Headlines should be:

, large  
, sharp  
, confident  
, easy to scan  
, not overcomplicated  
, not too poetic  

Body copy should be:

, short  
, clear  
, business focused  
, readable on dark background  
, not bloated  

Buttons should be:

, clear  
, direct  
, action focused  

## Layout Direction

The layout should use:

, strong grids  
, generous spacing  
, editorial composition  
, asymmetric moments where useful  
, clear section boundaries  
, clean content grouping  
, strong vertical rhythm  
, mobile first responsiveness  

Avoid:

, cramped sections  
, equal looking blocks everywhere  
, generic card grids  
, random floating elements  
, weak section transitions  
, overstuffed content  

## Page Atmosphere

The site should feel like a premium digital command center, not a basic agency site.

Possible atmosphere cues:

, dark canvas  
, red signal accents  
, subtle grid lines  
, soft glow accents  
, editorial text blocks  
, sharp cards  
, technical labels  
, structured layouts  
, premium motion  
, high contrast CTAs  

Avoid cheap neon overload.

Avoid futuristic effects that look like a gaming template.

## Component Design Rules

### Header

The header should feel minimal, sharp, and premium.

It should include:

, logo  
, primary navigation  
, clear CTA  
, mobile menu  

Rules:

, keep it clean  
, make the CTA visible  
, avoid crowded nav  
, use strong spacing  
, mobile menu must feel premium  

### Hero

The hero must communicate the business value immediately.

It should include:

, strong conversion focused headline  
, short strategic subtext  
, primary CTA  
, secondary CTA  
, trust or capability strip  
, visual system moment  

The hero should not rely only on a pretty background.

The headline must carry the page.

### Trust Strip

The trust strip should quickly communicate the studio’s core capabilities.

Possible items:

, CRO Strategy  
, UI/UX Design  
, Front End Build  
, SEO/GEO/AEO  
, Analytics Setup  

It should feel like a credibility layer, not a logo wall.

### Problem Section

The problem section should make the visitor feel understood.

It should expose why most websites fail:

, unclear positioning  
, weak conversion flow  
, generic visuals  
, poor tracking  
, no SEO structure  
, no growth loop  

This section should be direct and slightly sharp.

### Offer Cards

Offer cards should feel strategic, not basic service boxes.

Each offer card should include:

, offer name  
, short description  
, what it solves  
, what the visitor gets  
, CTA or anchor link if needed  

Core offers:

, Conversion Landing Pages  
, Premium Studio Websites  
, Analytics and Growth Systems  

### Process Section

The process should reduce risk.

It should show that Flammin has a system.

Suggested flow:

1. Diagnose
2. Design Direction
3. Build
4. Launch and Improve

Each step should feel practical, not vague.

### Selected Work

Case study cards should show business thinking.

Each case study should include:

, project name  
, category  
, problem  
, solution  
, stack or capability  
, outcome or business function  

No fake metrics.

If real metrics are unavailable, use operational outcomes.

### Founder Focused Section

This section should speak directly to founders, operators, and growth teams.

It should communicate:

, credibility before investor conversations  
, trust before sales calls  
, landing pages before campaigns  
, sharper positioning before scaling  
, better analytics before decisions  

### Audit CTA Section

This should be one of the strongest conversion moments on the page.

It should feel direct and valuable.

Main message:

> Get a sharp 7 point review of what is blocking trust, clarity, and conversion on your website.

CTA:

> Book a Conversion Audit

### FAQ

FAQ should support:

, objections  
, SEO  
, AEO  
, trust  
, clarity  

It should answer practical buying questions.

### Footer

Footer should feel premium and intentional.

It should include:

, short studio positioning  
, navigation  
, contact  
, social links  
, legal links  

Avoid generic WordPress style footer layouts.

## Motion Direction

Motion should support clarity, rhythm, and conversion.

Allowed motion:

, smooth section reveals  
, premium hover states  
, subtle background movement  
, mobile menu transition  
, case study card interactions  
, CTA hover elevation  
, scroll based storytelling only where useful  
, GSAP only for high value moments  

Avoid:

, random animations  
, heavy parallax  
, over animated text  
, motion that delays reading  
, motion that distracts from CTAs  
, template animation presets  
, animation on every element  

Motion should feel controlled and premium.

If motion does not improve attention, comprehension, trust, or conversion, do not use it.

## Mobile Direction

Mobile is critical.

The mobile version should not feel like a compressed desktop page.

Rules:

, clear hero hierarchy  
, visible CTA  
, readable font sizes  
, generous spacing  
, simple nav  
, strong vertical rhythm  
, no horizontal overflow  
, no tiny cards  
, no cramped sections  
, forms must be easy to use  

Mobile should feel designed, not patched.

## Responsive Direction

Design must work from:

```txt
320px mobile
480px large mobile
768px tablet
1024px laptop
1280px desktop
1440px large desktop
```

Use fluid spacing and responsive typography where possible.

Avoid fixed widths that break on smaller screens.

## Visual Anti Patterns

Do not use:

, generic glassmorphism cards everywhere  
, random abstract blobs  
, basic SaaS hero layout  
, floating dashboard mockup unless strategically justified  
, fake client logos  
, meaningless icon grids  
, stock looking 3D assets  
, noisy gradients  
, unreadable low contrast text  
, tiny body copy  
, weak CTA contrast  
, excessive borders  
, heavy shadows everywhere  

## Inspiration Logic

Use competitor references for pattern extraction only.

Do not copy layouts directly.

Benchmark logic:

, Orbit Media for proof and CRO maturity  
, Conversion Factory for direct offer clarity  
, Amply for startup and founder language  
, Refokus for premium visual confidence  
, Studio Freight for editorial personality  
, Locomotive for motion quality  

Flammin must have its own visual identity.

## Design Success Criteria

The design succeeds if:

, the hero is clear within 5 seconds  
, the site feels premium immediately  
, the CTA path is obvious  
, the layout feels custom, not templated  
, mobile feels polished  
, motion improves the experience  
, services feel strategic  
, selected work feels credible  
, the site supports SEO and analytics  
, the visitor understands why Flammin is different  

## Final Direction

Flammin Studio V2 should feel like:

, a premium conversion studio  
, a sharp web strategy partner  
, a front end execution partner  
, a founder ready digital partner  
, a studio with taste and performance discipline  

It should not feel like:

, a freelancer portfolio  
, a generic agency template  
, a trendy dark landing page  
, an animation playground  
, a website with no conversion logic
