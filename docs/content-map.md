# 🔥 Flammin Studio V2, Content Map

## Purpose

This document defines the content structure, section intent, message hierarchy, and conversion logic for the Flammin Studio V2 homepage.

This is not the final copy deck.

This is the strategic content map that guides the homepage build.

The goal is to make every section purposeful, clear, conversion focused, and aligned with the Flammin Studio positioning.

## Core Homepage Objective

The homepage must quickly explain:

, what Flammin Studio does  
, who it helps  
, why the work matters  
, why the visitor should trust it  
, what action the visitor should take next  

The homepage should move visitors toward one primary action:

> Book a Conversion Audit

Secondary action:

> View Selected Work

## Core Message

Flammin Studio builds conversion focused websites, landing pages, and digital systems for founders, service brands, and growth teams that need sharper positioning, stronger UX, and measurable lead flow.

## Homepage Positioning Thesis

The homepage should not position Flammin Studio as a regular web design service.

It should position Flammin Studio as a conversion focused web studio that connects strategy, UI/UX, front end development, SEO/GEO/AEO, analytics, and lead generation.

## Primary Audience

The homepage should speak to:

, startup founders  
, service brand owners  
, growth teams  
, lean marketing teams  
, agencies looking for execution partners  
, operators preparing for campaigns, launches, investor conversations, or market expansion  

## Primary Visitor Problem

Most websites fail because they look acceptable but do not perform as business systems.

Common problems:

, unclear positioning  
, weak first impression  
, generic visuals  
, poor conversion flow  
, no clear CTA path  
, weak mobile experience  
, poor tracking setup  
, weak SEO/GEO/AEO structure  
, no clear proof  
, no post launch learning loop  

## Desired Visitor Belief

After scanning the homepage, the visitor should believe:

, Flammin Studio understands business strategy  
, Flammin Studio is not just a design provider  
, Flammin Studio can make a website feel premium and perform better  
, Flammin Studio understands conversion, search, analytics, and user flow  
, Flammin Studio can move faster and sharper than a bloated agency  
, booking a conversion audit is a useful next step

## Section 6, Process Section

### Purpose

Reduce buyer risk by showing a clear working system.

### Section Headline Direction

> A focused process from diagnosis to launch.

Alternative:

> No random design. No bloated agency process. Just structured execution.

### Process Steps

### 1. Diagnose

Review the current website, offer, audience, friction points, competitors, conversion path, SEO foundation, and analytics gaps.

### 2. Design Direction

Define the structure, visual system, CTA hierarchy, layout rhythm, and user flow before building.

### 3. Build

Create the responsive front end with clean code, performance focus, SEO structure, and intentional motion.

### 4. Launch and Improve

Connect forms, tracking, QA, deploy, review behavior, and identify what to improve next.

### Content Notes

This section should feel practical and mature.

Avoid vague process words like discover, dream, create magic.

## Section 10, FAQ

### Purpose

Support objections, SEO, AEO, and buyer clarity.

### Suggested Questions

Answer direction:

Flammin Studio handles both the design direction and the front end build, with strategy, UX, SEO structure, and analytics considered from the beginning.

### Who is Flammin Studio best for?

Answer direction:

Founders, service brands, startups, growth teams, and businesses that need a sharper website or landing page built around trust, clarity, and conversion.

### Can you improve an existing website?

Answer direction:

Yes. The process can start with a conversion audit, then move into redesign, landing page creation, SEO structure, or tracking improvements.

### Do you handle SEO and analytics?

Answer direction:

Yes. The site can include SEO/GEO/AEO foundations, GTM, GA4, Search Console readiness, and conversion event planning.

### How fast can a landing page launch?

Answer direction:

Timeline depends on scope, content, and assets, but landing pages are designed to move faster than full website builds.

### Do you work internationally?

Answer direction:

Yes. Flammin Studio can work with local and international clients, especially founders, service brands, and growth teams.
