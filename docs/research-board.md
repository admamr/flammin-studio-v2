# 🔥 Flammin Studio V2, Research Board

## Purpose

This research board defines the strategic direction for the Flammin Studio V2 rebuild.

The goal is not to copy competitor websites.

The goal is to extract what works from high performing studio, CRO, startup web, and premium creative websites, then turn those patterns into a stronger Flammin Studio system.

Flammin Studio should become a premium conversion focused studio website for founders, service brands, startups, and growth teams.

## Core Research Insight

The strongest websites in this category do not sell “web design.”

They sell:

, clearer positioning  
, stronger conversion flow  
, better customer trust  
, sharper brand perception  
, measurable growth support  
, SEO and analytics visibility  
, faster go to market execution

Flammin Studio should not be framed as a regular web design service.

Flammin Studio should be framed as a studio that turns websites and landing pages into conversion systems.

## Strategic Category

Flammin Studio should sit between:

, startup website strategy  
, conversion focused landing pages  
, premium UI/UX execution  
, front end development  
, SEO/GEO/AEO foundations  
, analytics and tracking systems

The strongest positioning direction:

> Flammin Studio builds conversion focused websites, landing pages, and digital systems for founders, service brands, and growth teams that need sharper positioning, stronger UX, and measurable lead flow.

## Research Lanes

### 1. CRO and Website Optimization Agencies

Focus:

, conversion promise  
, analytics language  
, testing logic  
, proof and metrics  
, CTA structure  
, clarity over decoration

References:

, Orbit Media  
, Conversion.com  
, Conversion Factory

Key insight:

CRO websites win when they connect design decisions to business outcomes. The strongest ones do not separate design from analytics, SEO, UX, research, and measurable performance.

Flammin takeaway:

Use CRO logic as the business foundation of the site. Design should be presented as a conversion tool, not decoration.

### 2. Startup and SaaS Website Agencies

Focus:

, founder facing language  
, investor readiness  
, scalable marketing websites  
, fast launch positioning  
, startup credibility  
, productized services

References:

, Amply  
, BRIX Agency  
, Flow Ninja  
, Baunfire

Key insight:

Startup focused website agencies sell clarity, speed, credibility, and growth readiness. The buyer is usually not just buying a website. They are buying trust before a funding round, launch, campaign, or sales push.

Flammin takeaway:

The homepage should speak to founders and operators who need to look credible before their next serious business move.

### 3. Premium Creative Studios

Focus:

, visual taste  
, motion rhythm  
, typography  
, grid systems  
, editorial structure  
, brand memorability

References:

, Refokus  
, Studio Freight  
, Locomotive  
, Instrument  
, Awwwards agency references

Key insight:

Premium creative studios create emotional trust through taste, restraint, composition, and memorable visual systems.

Flammin takeaway:

The site should feel visually distinct and premium, but not at the cost of clarity.

### 4. Solo or Small Studio Trust Builders

Focus:

, how smaller teams look premium  
, how expertise is framed as an advantage  
, how service pages build confidence  
, how proof works without huge brand logos  
, how to avoid looking like a freelancer portfolio

References:

, premium independent studios  
, senior product designer portfolios  
, boutique CRO consultants  
, independent Webflow or Framer experts

Key insight:

A smaller studio can look more premium than a large agency if the point of view is clear, the case studies are framed properly, and the offer feels focused.

Flammin takeaway:

Flammin should not hide that it is lean. It should turn that into a strength: sharper execution, direct strategy, less agency bloat, faster movement.
